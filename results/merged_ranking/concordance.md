## Tier violations and within-tier flips (honest statement)

1. **T3 vs T4 is NOT separated** (sign test p = 1.0): restatements do not reliably beat strategizing scaffolds. The failures are real backfires of restatements, not noise: the auction menu moves GPT-4o AWAY from truthful play (rho = -0.39, Welch p = 0.003 in auction_cells.csv) and menu-mechanics backfires in DA for GPT-4o (-3.17) and Gemma (-4.32). The pre-committed tier claim 'baseline ~ B1' survives only as the two-sided placebo test (3/8 above zero, p = 0.73) -- i.e., B1 is null ON AVERAGE but heterogeneous across models and variants.
2. **B1 splits in DA**: the invariance-property menu behaves like a B2 safety description (pooled rho = +0.65; significant improvements for Claude and Gemini, MW p <= 1e-4 in da_cells_summary.md), while the mechanics-only menu backfires (pooled rho = -1.67). The figure therefore shows three B1 rows rather than asserting one 'menu' rung.
3. **Within-tier domain flip (expected, plan §4)**: payoff tree > safety in 3/4 auction cells, safety > tree in 4/4 DA cells -- consistent with keeping B2/B3/C1 in ONE tier with no within-tier order asserted.
4. **Axis-2 baseline provenance (resolved, 2026-07-09)**: the V12 `axis2_forward_baseline` template was NOT a plain SPSB text -- it was a two-stage sealed-bid-as-clock-exit description (a Breitmoser-style clock-framing/B3 variant), so it is a TREATED cell, not a baseline (see results/merged_ranking/_axis2_baseline_provenance.md; trace check: 15-25% of claude/gemini/gemma plans under this cell echo clock/exit/stage-2 language, vs 0% under axis1/axis3). Gemma's formerly 'anomalously good axis-2 baseline' (SMAD 8.6% vs axis1/axis3 ~24-29%) is explained by that framing partly fixing Gemma's play, not by baseline noise. Corrected canon: the pooled axis baseline is {axis1, axis3} only, and axis-2 treatments (safety, tree, lookahead) are now contrasted against that corrected pool. On the corrected pool every Gemma axis-2 auction rho is POSITIVE (safety +0.34, tree +0.59, lookahead +0.47) -- the earlier 'baseline-noise-driven backfire' reading is retired.
5. **OSP-DA rho = 1.0 is censored**: the tau metric records zero error by construction on length-1 revealed sequences; the E9 decision-level accounting (da_cells_summary.md) still finds 0 misreports for 3/4 models in the pick protocol (rule-of-three 95% UBs ~0.9%) but Gemma has 5 strict pick errors (1.6%) and Claude has 24.1% Type-1 false rejections in the yes/no tree -- '0.0%' is protocol-specific.
6. **DA rho magnitudes are ratio-unstable for GPT-4o**: its DA direct_baseline error is only 1.0%, so any treatment error of a few percent produces extreme negative rho (risk-averse -20.2, lookahead -3.8). Ranks are unaffected; clipped in the figure at -1.5.
7. **Risk-seeking persona (not a figure row)** is heterogeneous, not uniformly bad: Claude overbids catastrophically in auctions (mean dev +8.40, SMAD 35.0% vs risk-neutral 7.6%) while GPT-4o's DA play is nearly unaffected (0.7% tau error). The figure keeps the canonical D rung (risk-averse) only.
8. **C2 condition choice**: the DA lookahead mark is axis2_1step (the paper's 'one-step' 7.8% cell). Deeper scaffolds are not monotone (2step 5.1%, fullsim pooled ~3.8%), so the C2 rung is sensitive to k; the auction mark (axis2_forward_backward_induct) is a fake-sequential framing since SPSB has no true forward-planning axis (docs/INTERVENTION_TAXONOMY.md).

## Sensitivity and honest caveats

Under the corrected-baseline canon, the axis-2 treatments (safety, tree, lookahead) are contrasted against the pooled axis baseline {axis1, axis3} -- `axis2_forward_baseline` is a treated clock-exit cell and is excluded (point 4 above; _axis2_baseline_provenance.md). Only the belief and risk cells retain their own axis-specific baselines (axis3_beliefs_baseline, risk_neutrality). Belief cells whose rho sign flips if their axis-specific baseline is swapped for the pooled axis baseline:

- **Second-order beliefs / Claude 3.5 Haiku (auction)**: axis-specific baseline gives rho = -0.16, pooled baseline gives +0.04 (sign flips; axis baseline SMAD 6.8 vs pooled 8.3).
- **Second-order beliefs / Gemini 2.0 Flash (auction)**: axis-specific baseline gives rho = +0.24, pooled baseline gives -0.04 (sign flips; axis baseline SMAD 7.0 vs pooled 5.1).
