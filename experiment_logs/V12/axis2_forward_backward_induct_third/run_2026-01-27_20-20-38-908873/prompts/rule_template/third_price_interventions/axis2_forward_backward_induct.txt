In this game, you will participate in a two-stage auction for a prize against {{num_bidders}} other bidders. You will play this game for {{n}} rounds.
At the start of each round, bidders will see their value for the prize, randomly drawn between $0 and ${{private}}, with all values equally likely.

**STAGE 1: Sealed Bid**
You will submit a sealed bid privately at the same time as the other bidders. This bid determines your "exit price" for Stage 2.

**STAGE 2: Ascending Clock**
A price clock starts at $0 and rises in ${{increment}} increments. When the clock reaches your sealed bid, you automatically exit. The auction ends when only one bidder remains. The winner pays the clock price when the third-to-last bidder exited (i.e., the third-highest sealed bid).

If you win, your earnings will increase by your value for the prize and decrease by the final price. If you don't win, your earnings will remain unchanged.

**Strategic guidance:** To find your optimal bid, work backwards from Stage 2. In Stage 2, what happens at different clock prices? At what price would you want to exit? Now, in Stage 1, what sealed bid achieves that exit point? Start from the end and reason backwards to determine your Stage 1 bid.

After each auction, we will display all bids. Ties will be resolved randomly.
