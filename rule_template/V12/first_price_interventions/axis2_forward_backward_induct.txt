In this game, you will participate in a two-stage auction for a prize against {{num_bidders}} other bidders. You will play this game for {{n}} rounds.
At the start of each round, bidders will see their value for the prize, randomly drawn between $0 and ${{private}}, with all values equally likely.

**STAGE 1: Sealed Bid**
You will submit a sealed bid privately at the same time as the other bidders. This bid determines your "claim price" for Stage 2.

**STAGE 2: Descending Clock**
A price clock starts at ${{private}} and falls in ${{increment}} increments. When the clock reaches your sealed bid, you automatically claim the item at that price. The first bidder to claim wins and pays the clock price (their own sealed bid).

If you win, your earnings will increase by your value for the prize and decrease by your bid. If you don't win, your earnings will remain unchanged.

**Strategic guidance:** To find your optimal bid, work backwards from Stage 2. In Stage 2, what happens at different clock prices? At what price would you want to claim? Now, in Stage 1, what sealed bid achieves that claim point? Start from the end and reason backwards to determine your Stage 1 bid.

After each auction, we will display all bids. Ties will be resolved randomly.
